About Us

Meet the team connecting your passion for flying with the world

Our Mission

Learn aviation. Speak aviation. Fly anywhere.

At AeroBridge we believe every pilot deserves the same opportunities as their international colleagues. Our mission is to remove the language and bureaucratic barriers that keep aviators from global success.

We are the bridge between your local training and certifications recognised worldwide. We do not just prepare you for an examination, we prepare you for a career without borders.

Global Vision

To be the Latin American reference for training and certifying pilots who want to expand their professional horizons beyond borders.

Experience in Aviation and Education

Sebastian is a graduate in Aviation and Aerospace from Metropolitan State University of Denver (MSU Denver), one of the leading aviation universities in the United States. During his studies he worked directly in FAA-regulated flight operations at Frontier Airlines, supporting more than 200 daily flights from the operations centre.

With experience at International Jet, where he coordinated private charter and high-priority medical flights (including organ transport), Sebastian developed deep knowledge of aviation regulations, dispatch logistics, and international communications.

As an instructor and tutor at MSU Denver, Sebastian taught aviation students and helped them prepare for their certifications. That direct teaching experience is the foundation of the AeroBridge approach: personalised classes, practical material, and real support.

Sebastian is a native English speaker with B2 Spanish, which allows him to teach aviation and general English with full command of the technical content and to bridge communication between Latin American students and the international aviation world.

What Sebastian teaches at AeroBridge:

Classes de ingles general (todos los niveles) — Ingles aeronautico y fraseologia ATC — Preparacion para examenes ICAO Nivel 4, 5 y 6 — Preparacion para examenes FAA (PPL, IFR, CPL, ATP) — Advisory for studying at flight schools in the USA, Canada, and Europe
