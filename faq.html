Certifications FAA e ICAO

Depending on your current English level and previous experience, preparation can take 2 to 6 months. We create a personalised plan around your specific needs and availability.

FAA (Federal Aviation Administration): The United States certification, recognised worldwide and highly valued by international airlines.

ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization): The international language proficiency standard required for international operations. It measures your aviation English communication ability on a scale of 1 to 6.

We recommend at least an intermediate level (B1) to begin certification preparation. We also offer general and aviation English courses for students who need to strengthen their foundation first.

Our students achieve excellent results thanks to our personalised methodology, current material, and continuous progress tracking.
