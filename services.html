General

AES 2050 — Aviation and Aerospace History

AES 3220 — Aviation and Aerospace Law

AES 4910 — Strategic Planning

Operations

AES 2130 — Commercial Flight Operations
2 chapters, 4 lessons · Complex systems and aeronautical decision making. Prepares for the FAA Commercial Knowledge test.

AES 3230 — Airline Management

Navigation

4 courses · Instruments, IFR, and advanced systems

AES 1710 — Instrument Flight Simulation I
2 chapters, 5 lessons · Flight instruments, VOR/GPS navigation, and weight and balance.
AES 2120 — Instrument Fundamentals
2 chapters, 5 lessons · IFR procedures and VOR/ILS/GPS navigation. Prepares for the FAA Instrument Knowledge test.
AES 2710 — Instrument Flight Simulation II
2 chapters, 5 lessons · IFR planning, ATC clearances, holding patterns, and approaches.
AES 4370 — Advanced Navigation Systems
2 chapters, 4 lessons · INS/IRS, FMS, RAIM, EFIS, and synthetic vision systems.

Technical

AES 1040 — Introduction to UAS
3 chapters, 8 lessons · UAS operations, control stations, payloads, and FAA Part 107.
AES 1050 — Introduction to Space
2 chapters, 4 lessons · Orbital mechanics and practical satellite design.
AES 3000 — Aircraft Systems and Propulsion
2 chapters, 4 lessons · Piston and turbine engines, hydraulic, pneumatic, and electrical systems.
AES 3530 — Aerodynamics
3 chapters, 6 lessons · Airfoil theory, lift, drag, and stability from subsonic to supersonic.
AES 4040 — Aircraft Performance
2 chapters, 4 lessons · V-speeds, takeoff and landing distances, and performance calculations.
AES 4935 — Advanced Commercial Aircraft Systems
2 chapters, 4 lessons · Turbofan, fly-by-wire, TCAS, EGPWS, and emergency procedures.

Certification

5 courses · From private pilot to ATP

AES 1500 — Private Flight
2 chapters, 4 lessons · FAA Private Pilot certificate under Part 61 or 141. Minimum 35 flight hours.
AES 2500 — Instrument Flight
