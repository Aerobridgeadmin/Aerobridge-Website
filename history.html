Our History

The road that led us to connect pilots with the world

The Origin

It all began with a dream

AeroBridge was born from personal experience: the frustration of watching extraordinarily talented pilots see their careers limited by language barriers and a lack of guidance on international certifications.

Our founder decided to build the resource he wished he had: a bridge connecting excellent local aviation training with global opportunities.

"I saw too many incredibly talented colleagues left behind simply because nobody showed them the way. AeroBridge exists to change that."

· AeroBridge Founder

Timeline

Our journey

2020

The Beginning

In the middle of the pandemic, when many pilots saw their careers on hold, AeroBridge started as a light of hope. We began teaching aviation English to a small group of pilots who wanted to make the most of the time.

2021

First Certifications

Our first 50 students earned their ICAO and FAA certifications. We began building our reputation as trusted advisors and formalised our preparation programs.

2022

International Expansion

We established partnerships with flight schools in the United States and Canada, launched our study-abroad advisory service, and passed 150 certified students.

2023

Consolidation

We grew the team with more certified instructors, reached 350 certified students, and began welcoming students from across Latin America.

2024

New Horizons

We expanded our services, launched specialised programs for different licence types, and established new partnerships in Europe and Oceania.

2025

The Future

We keep innovating: the AeroBridge training platform, an international source library, and country packs so every pilot can reach their full potential anywhere in the world.

Our vision for tomorrow

We dream of a future where no Latin American pilot sees their career limited by language barriers or lack of information about international opportunities.
